# Scoring Methodology

Explainable 0–100 screening heuristic:

- Surface-condition pressure: 25%
- Weather exposure pressure: 17%
- Usage and capacity pressure: 20%
- Equipment-control pressure: 18%
- Incident-history pressure: 20%

Bands:
- Low: 0–24.9
- Moderate: 25–49.9
- High: 50–74.9
- Critical: 75–100

The score is a planning signal, not a clinical, legal or regulatory determination.
