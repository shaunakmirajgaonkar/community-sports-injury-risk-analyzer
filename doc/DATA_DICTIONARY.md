# Data Dictionary

### Surface conditions
Facility surface type, condition score, worn-area pressure, puddle pressure, inspection recency, maintenance backlog and cleanliness.

### Weather conditions
Temperature, humidity, rainfall, heat exposure, weather variability and wind exposure.

### Facility usage
Sport type, daily and peak users, weekly sessions, utilization, session duration and capacity pressure.

### Equipment checks
Check schedules, completion, equipment issues, protective-equipment readiness, goal-post checks, lighting, and maintenance backlog.

### Incident reports
Incident identity/date, sport type, severity, reported risk factor, reported injury, near-miss, body area and 30-day local incident count.
